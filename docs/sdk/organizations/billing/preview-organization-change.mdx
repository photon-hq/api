`POST /v1/organizations/{organizationId}/billing/preview`

Previews a fixed-charge quantity change on the selected organization's own subscription, such as SSO. Prices the change by the billing engine exactly as making it would bill it now: dueNow is that invoice, with taxes and the payer's credits applied. A purchase bills each unit it adds, even after a release this period. A release bills nothing; releasing a prorated charge credits its unused days to the organization's credit. dueLater is what the change leads to that is billed later instead, and nextCharge what is charged in advance at the next period start. change says what the change does by its kind: for a fixed-charge change, pass change.quote on with the purchase so it is refused if the price or period no longer hold. This operation does not change billing.

## Parameters

<ParamField path="organizationId" type={"string"} required>

</ParamField>


## Body

<ParamField body="fixedCharge" type={"FixedChargeChange"} required>
A quantity change made now, as a purchase or release makes it.
</ParamField>


## Returns

**`200`** What the change would bill. PreviewOrganizationChangeResponse

<ResponseField name="balanceAfterCents" type={"integer | null"} required>
The payer's credit balance once dueNow is charged: the credit the change issues included, less what dueNow uses. In dueNow's currency. Null when the change charges nothing now (a downgrade).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="change" type={"BillingChangeEffect"} required>
What the change does beside what it bills, by the kind of change requested: fixed_charge for a fixed-charge change, plan_change for a plan change.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="dueLater" type={"BillingPreviewDeferred"} required>
Billed later rather than with dueNow: one line per plan change addition, billed when it is added and priced as if added now. Before taxes; the balance left after dueNow applies to it first. nextCharge already includes it. No lines when nothing is billed later.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="dueNow" type={"BillingPreviewInvoice"} required>
Charged when the change is made, priced as the change bills it. No lines when it charges nothing now.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="nextCharge" type={"BillingNextCharge | null"} required>
What is charged in advance at the next period start for what is held after the change. Usage and a minimum commitment are billed after the period. Null when nothing is, or the subscription ends first.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="timeZone" type={"string"} required>
The IANA time zone the payer is billed in, e.g. America/Los_Angeles. Billing periods start and end at its midnights, so read their calendar dates in it.
</ResponseField>
