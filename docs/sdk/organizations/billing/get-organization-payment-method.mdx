`GET /v1/organizations/{organizationId}/billing/payment-method`

Reports whether the selected organization has a card on file, its documented payment-method metadata, whether a purchase needs a card first (purchaseRefusal), and how it pays: by card, or by invoice on its contract's terms (agreement). Reading this endpoint does not collect a new card or create a checkout session.

## Returns

**`200`** Whether the organization has a captured card and its metadata. GetOrganizationPaymentMethodResponse

Headers: `ETag`, `Photon-Version`. The raw response carries these headers; see [Raw responses](/api-client/raw-responses).

<ResponseField name="agreement" type={"BillingAgreement"} required>
How the organization pays: self_serve by card, each purchase paid when made; enterprise by invoice on its contract's terms, its purchases added to its next monthly invoice.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="cardOnFile" type={"boolean"} required>
True once a card is captured for the payer.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="paymentMethod" type={"PaymentMethod | null"} required>
Metadata for the default card, or null.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="purchaseRefusal" type={"PurchaseRefusal | null"} required>
Why the organization can't pay for a purchase now, or null when it can. payment_method_required: add a card first; an organization that pays by invoice never needs one. billing_not_ready: its billing is still being set up; try again shortly. Decide purchases on this, not on cardOnFile.
</ResponseField>

**`304`** Not Modified. Sent only when If-None-Match matches the ETag of an earlier 200 response for the same representation; no body. No body.

Headers: `ETag`, `Photon-Version`. The raw response carries these headers; see [Raw responses](/api-client/raw-responses).
