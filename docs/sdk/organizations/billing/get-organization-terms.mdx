`GET /v1/organizations/{organizationId}/billing/terms`

Returns what the selected organization is billed on now on its own subscription, such as SSO and 10DLC campaigns: the plan as the subscription has it, with any override applied, each fixed charge at the subscription's price and the quantity the organization holds, and resolved entitlements; where the plan comes from; how invoices are paid; the current period and next billing date; and any downgrade or cancellation waiting for the period end. subscription has a project subscription's terms without its category, and is null when the organization holds none. Project subscriptions are read per project. This operation does not change billing.

## Returns

**`200`** The organization's effective billing terms. GetOrganizationTermsResponse

<ResponseField name="subscription" type={"OrganizationSubscriptionTerms | null"} required>
What one subscription is billed on now. plan is the plan as this subscription has it: its own prices when overridden, each fixed charge at the subscription's price and quantity, and entitlements resolved with overrides. The organization's own subscription, or null when it holds none.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="timeZone" type={"string"} required>
The IANA time zone the payer is billed in, e.g. America/Los_Angeles. Billing periods start and end at its midnights, so read their calendar dates in it.
</ResponseField>
